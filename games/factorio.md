## Layout-Vorschlag Factorio (nur linke Hand)

```
TOP(0-5):     ESC  1    W    E    R    T
HOME(12-17):  Q    A    S    D    F    G
BOTTOM(24-29):LSH  Z    B    C    X    V
THUMB(36-38): LCTRL  SPACE  LALT
```

| Pos | Taste | Finger | Funktion |
|-----|-------|--------|----------|
| 0 | ESC | Pinky ↑ | Menü |
| 1 | 1 | Ring ↑ | Waffe 1 |
| 2 | **W** | Mitte ↑ | Vorwärts |
| 3 | **E** | Zeige ↑ | Inventar |
| 4 | **R** | Zeige ↑↗ | Rotieren |
| 5 | T | inner ↑ | Chat |
| 12 | **Q** | Pinky ═ | Pipette |
| 13 | **A** | Ring ═ | Links |
| 14 | **S** | Mitte ═ | Rückwärts |
| 15 | **D** | Zeige ═ | Rechts |
| 16 | F | Zeige ═↗ | Interagieren |
| 17 | G | inner ═ | Fahrzeug-GUI |
| 24 | LSHIFT | Pinky ↓ | Rennen |
| 25 | Z | Ring ↓ | Drop |
| 26 | **B** | Mitte ↓ | Blueprints |
| 27 | C | Zeige ↓ | Copy (Ctrl+C) |
| 28 | X | Zeige ↓↗ | Cut (Ctrl+X) |
| 29 | V | inner ↓ | Paste (Ctrl+V) |
| 36 | LCTRL | Daumen | Ctrl-Mod |
| 37 | SPACE | Daumen | Springen/Schießen |
| 38 | LALT | Daumen | Alt-Mode |

### Ctrl-Kombos

| Aktion | Eingabe |
|--------|---------|
| **Undo** (Ctrl+Z) | LCTRL + Z |
| **Copy** (Ctrl+C) | LCTRL + C |
| **Cut** (Ctrl+X) | LCTRL + X |
| **Paste** (Ctrl+V) | LCTRL + V |

### Fehlende Vs. rechte Hand / Combo

| Funktion | Lösung |
|----------|--------|
| **M** (Karte) | Rechte Hälfte oder Combo |
| **TAB** (Waffe) | Rechter Daumen pos 40 |
| **Alt+LSH** | LALT + LSHIFT — kurz beide Daumen |
